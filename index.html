Gestures

  • DragA short flick is one bond. Keep going for a zig-zag chain. Start on an atom to grow from it.
  • Draw a loopA circle makes a six-membered ring. Draw 3 to 8 corners for other sizes. Loop over a bond to fuse.
  • Circle inside a ringAdds alternating double bonds. Do it again to remove them. Tapping inside a ring works too.
  • Tap a bondSingle, double, triple. Hold, then let go, for wedges and hashes.
  • Tap an atomOpens the element wheel: N, O, S, halogens, charges, or type any symbol.
  • ScribbleErases whatever it covers.
  • Hold, then dragMoves an atom. Hold a bond to move the whole molecule.
  • Two-finger tapUndo (three fingers redo). Pinch or drag with two fingers to zoom and pan.

Hover an atom and press N O S F P C H, L Cl, B Br, I, +/− for charge. Hover a bond: 1 2 3, W wedge, H hash. Del deletes. Scroll or space-drag to pan; mouse wheel or Ctrl‑scroll to zoom; 0 fits.
